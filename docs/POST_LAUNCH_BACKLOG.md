# PERUM Post-Launch Backlog

> Этот scope сохранён, но не блокирует Web-first Launch V1 и не входит в launch
> progress. Актуальные launch status и очередь находятся только в
> [PRODUCT_MASTER_PLAN.md](PRODUCT_MASTER_PLAN.md).

## Правила Возврата

Задача возвращается в active roadmap только когда:

- Launch V1 достиг 100% либо задача стала доказанным P0/P1 launch blocker;
- определены владелец, пользовательский outcome и exit criteria;
- закрыты обязательные provider/legal/operator prerequisites;
- работа планируется целым vertical slice, а не отдельным endpoint или экраном.

## Mobile Expansion

- Full Student Mobile economy: market purchases, equipment и exchange mutations.
- Parent mutations, exports и полная transaction history.
- Teacher full journal, works mutations, reports и offline conflict recovery.
- School Admin/Director CRUD, rosters, schedule mutations и contact actions.
- Organization Admin и Platform Admin Native operational parity.
- Sensitive offline persistence beyond approved read-only academics.
- Signed Stage F lifecycle pilot как Mobile production gate.
- EAS signing, App Store/Google Play builds и store rollout.
- Universal links, app links и полная physical-device matrix.

## Push and Realtime

- Real APNs/FCM provider delivery и token lifecycle.
- Quiet hours, preference center и provider-level delivery analytics.
- Push deep links для всех ролей и событий.
- Background refresh beyond Launch V1 Web notifications.

## Media and Attachments

- Production activation ClamAV/scanner contour.
- Attachments для support, homework, chat и moderation.
- Target-node saturation, capacity sizing и multi-school fairness evidence.
- Media retention, deletion, quarantine и legal-hold operations.

Возврат разрешён только после approved target с минимум 8 GiB RAM, scanner
saturation proof и утверждённой retention/moderation policy.

## Social Expansion

- Group chats.
- Parent visibility/consent controls beyond Launch V1.
- Advanced anti-abuse, rate policy и moderation automation.
- Rich attachments и media previews.
- Advanced presence/realtime behavior.
- Social production rollout beyond controlled pilot.

## Billing Automation

- Полная YooKassa payment/refund automation, если Launch V1 использует manual flow.
- Fiscal receipt automation и expanded accounting integrations.
- Self-service tariff changes, promo codes и complex proration.
- Automated delinquency enforcement beyond non-destructive reconciliation.
- Advanced revenue analytics.

## Product Expansion

- Advanced Student economy, quests, exchange и inventory actions.
- Parent exports и expanded analytics.
- Teacher analytics reports и drill-down beyond launch journeys.
- Advanced school analytics and configurable dashboards.
- Non-critical visual polish and parity screens.

## Infrastructure Expansion

- Organization node HA и automated failover beyond accepted Launch RTO/RPO.
- Large-scale capacity automation beyond bounded two-school proof.
- Advanced release orchestration and fully automated operator pilots.
- Additional observability dashboards that do not close a Launch gate.
